## 1. Пластилін — кіт і квиток

Handmade claymation stop-motion style, visible fingerprints in the plasticine, slightly jerky stop-motion movement, warm practical lighting, miniature set. A chubby ginger clay cat lies bored on a clay sofa in a small living room, tail twitching. A blank paper ticket with a coral-red stub slides in under the front door. The cat's ears shoot up, it leaps off the sofa and chases the ticket, which skitters away like a mouse: across the rug, under a chair that topples, up a bookshelf sending clay books flying, around a floor lamp that wobbles, and out through the cat flap. The camera follows low and fast behind the cat as it bursts out of the cat flap into a clay evening street glowing with strings of lights, lanterns, a tiny stage with spotlights and a food cart steaming — and the cat skids to a stop, eyes wide, as more colourful tickets flutter down around it like confetti.

## 2. Піксельна гра — квиток-герой

Retro 16-bit pixel-art side-scrolling video game style, crisp chunky pixels, bright limited palette, parallax scrolling city background at dusk, smooth game animation. A small cute hero shaped like a walking paper ticket with a coral-red stub and tiny legs runs fast to the right through a pixel city, jumping over gaps and bouncing on awnings. It collects spinning coloured ticket-shaped coins that burst into pixel sparkles. It races through three quick zones without stopping: a concert zone with a pixel stage, pulsing spotlights and bouncing speakers; a food-market zone with steaming stalls and strings of bulbs; a theatre zone with red curtains that swing open. At the end it leaps off a ramp, grabs a big glowing golden ticket in mid-air and pixel fireworks explode across the night sky. No HUD, no score, no interface elements.

## 3. Аніме — квиток на вітрі

Hand-painted Japanese anime film style, lush painterly backgrounds, soft cel-shaded animation, golden-hour summer evening light, drifting clouds. On a balcony with laundry and potted plants, a gust of wind lifts a single blank paper ticket with a teal stub off a small table. The camera flies with the ticket as the wind carries it on a sweeping journey over a painted hillside city: it tumbles past swaying power lines with startled sparrows, swoops over tiled rooftops and a sleeping cat, races alongside a little commuter train crossing a bridge, dives through the spray of a fountain making a rainbow, and whirls down into a lantern-lit evening festival street with paper lanterns, glowing stalls and drifting sparks, where hundreds of other tickets rise up to dance around it in the warm air.

## 4. Мініатюра — дощ із пінів

Photoreal tilt-shift miniature diorama style, a tiny detailed model city on a tabletop at dusk with very shallow depth of field, toy-like cars and trams moving along the streets, tiny windows glowing. From high above, one big glossy coral-red map pin drops out of the sky and lands in a city square with a springy bounce and a ring of light, making the tiny trees shake. Then dozens more colourful map pins (coral red, mustard yellow, teal, violet) rain down across the whole city one after another like a hailstorm, each landing with a bounce and lighting up its block with a warm pulse, toy cars swerving around them. The camera sweeps fast and low between the buildings, weaving around the landing pins, then rockets upward to reveal the entire miniature city sparkling with pins.

## 5. Валяна вовна — пташка-листоноша

Needle-felted wool stop-motion animation style, soft fuzzy textures, handmade felt miniature world, cosy warm lighting, gentle but lively stop-motion movement. A small round felt bird wakes with a start on a felt branch, grabs a blank felt ticket with a mustard-yellow stub in its beak and takes off. The camera flies right behind the bird at speed over a felt city: it zips between fuzzy woollen chimneys puffing cotton smoke, dodges a felt kite, loops around a knitted clock tower, skims over a felt tram with wobbling wires, startles a row of felt pigeons into flight, and dives toward a cosy felt house where it lands on the windowsill and taps on the glass with its beak. The window lights up warm and the bird hops happily while more felt birds with colourful tickets arrive on neighbouring windowsills all along the street.

## 6. Неон — траса до нічного міста

Stylised low-poly 3D synthwave style, glowing neon outlines in coral, magenta, teal and violet on deep indigo, reflective wet road, light trails, retro-futuristic night. A blank ticket with a glowing coral stub rides flat like a hoverboard, skimming at high speed along a neon highway toward a low-poly night skyline. The camera chases close behind and beside it: it weaves between neon pylons, grinds along a glowing guard rail throwing sparks, launches off a ramp and does a slow barrel roll over a gap, lands and drifts through a long curve, then shoots through a tunnel of light rings. As it bursts out of the tunnel the whole city ahead switches on district by district in waves of neon, searchlights sweep the sky, and a swarm of other glowing tickets streams in from side roads to ride alongside it into the city.

## 7. Акварель — листопад розфарбовується

Loose watercolour storybook illustration style, visible paper grain, wet ink bleeding and blooming, hand-drawn wobbly line work, gently animated. A grey rainy November city street painted in washed-out greys: bare trees, puddles, drooping street lamps, fallen leaves. A small bright umbrella made of coloured ticket stubs (coral, mustard, teal, violet) pops open and bobs quickly down the street by itself. Wherever it passes, colour floods out from it like wet paint spreading on paper: grey houses bloom into warm colours, windows light up, bare trees burst into golden leaves, puddles turn into mirrors of coloured lights, garlands unroll between lamp posts. The camera hurries alongside the umbrella as the wave of colour races ahead of it up the whole street, across a square with a carousel that starts turning, and over the rooftops until the entire painted city glows with colour under the rain.

## 8. Кубики — місце збирається саме

Toy construction-block stop-motion style, a world built from small glossy plastic building blocks with studs, bright primary colours, snappy stop-motion assembly, tabletop macro with shallow depth of field. An empty grey baseplate. One small coral block drops from above and clicks into place. Then blocks rain in fast from every side and click together in rapid bursts, building themselves into a cosy little gathering spot: a long table, benches, a string of tiny lamps that light up, a small stage with speakers, a food cart with a striped awning, trees, bunting. The camera spins quickly around the build as it grows, ducking under the bunting as it snaps into place, then pulls back fast as more plots around it assemble themselves the same way — a whole neighbourhood of little gathering places popping up across the baseplate, lamps switching on one after another.

## 9. Фотореалізм — паніка біля входу

Photorealistic cinematic macro style, real textures, warm evening light outside a venue entrance with blurred bokeh lights in the background, handheld energy. A canvas tote bag sits on a ledge and suddenly starts shaking: things fly out of it one after another in a frantic search — keys, headphones, a lip balm, a crumpled scarf, a water bottle, coins rolling away, a tangle of chargers — everything tumbling through the air in fast chaotic arcs. Then several blank paper tickets with coloured stubs shoot up out of the bag, hang in the air for a beat, neatly line up in a row like a deck of cards being fanned, and zip one by one in a quick satisfying stream into a smartphone lying face down on the ledge, slotting under it with little snaps. The last ticket slides in, the phone gives a small happy hop, and the scattered things freeze in mid-air and drop.

## 10. Комікс — наввипередки з годинником

Bold 2D comic-book pop-art animation style, thick ink outlines, halftone dots, flat saturated colours, speed lines, dynamic panel transitions; all speech bubbles and panels contain only pictures, never writing. A little hero shaped like a blank ticket with a coral stub and a tiny cape dashes out of a concert hall doorway as stage lights flash behind it. Above the city hangs a huge round clock face with no numerals, its hands sweeping fast. The hero races across the page from panel to panel, smashing through the panel borders: sprinting along a night street, sliding down a stair rail, leaping onto the roof of a moving tram, swinging round a lamp post, jumping over a puddle with a big splash of halftone dots. The clock hands swing toward the top; with a final burst of speed lines the hero dives through a lit doorway of a cosy house just in time, the door swings shut, the window glows warm and a big starburst pops over the roof.
