Privacy Policy

In short: we keep only what is needed for you to find events, host your own and talk to attendees. We do not sell data and show no ads.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Taras Vovchenko, a private individual based in Ukraine ("we"). The app is free and not operated as a business. Privacy contact: hello@poriad.app. This policy covers the Poruch mobile app for Android and iOS and this website.

3. Legal bases

  • Contract (Terms of Use): account, events, attendance, chat, notifications.
  • Legitimate interest: security, abuse prevention, rate limiting, moderation.
  • Consent: location, push notifications, local reminders. Withdraw at any time in device or app settings.
  • Legal obligation: age verification and lawful requests from authorities.

5. Retention

  • Account and profile: until you delete the account.
  • Events, attendance, chat: while the event or account exists. Chat closes one week after the event ends.
  • Reports: up to 12 months after review.
  • Push tokens: until sign-out or device change.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.
  • Local cache and drafts on the device: until sign-out or app removal.

6. Your rights

  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or via the website instructions. Account, profile, attendance, drafts, tokens and messages are deleted; events you created are cancelled.
  • Access, rectify or restrict your data by emailing hello@poriad.app. We reply within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent for location and notifications in device settings.
  • Complain to the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights or, if you are in the EU, to your supervisory authority.

7. Security

All connections use TLS. Sessions are stored in Android Keystore and iOS Keychain. Server-side row-level security prevents access to other users' names, messages and queue positions even via direct API calls. Passwords are hashed. We will notify you of incidents that may affect you within 72 hours of discovery.

8. Children

Poruch is for adults. Sign-up requires being 18 or older, and age is checked at account creation. If you learn a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We announce material changes in the app at least 14 days before they take effect. The effective date is shown at the top.
