1. What Poriad is

Poriad is an app for finding city events on a map, hosting your own meetups and joining others. It is provided by Taras Vovchenko, a private individual based in Ukraine ("we"), free of charge and not as a business. Some events are imported from public listings and ticketing sites with their source shown; others are created by users.

We are not the organizer of any event, do not sell tickets and are not a party to arrangements between organizers and attendees. "Buy ticket" opens the source website; payment and refunds follow its rules.
